Rental Terms

  • Rental duration is calculated based on the selected rental period.
  • Standard daily rental includes the specified kilometres for each vehicle; extra KM billed per vehicle's rate.
  • Self-drive and driver options available depending on requirement; driver charges are separate.
  • Final vehicle availability must be confirmed by Samay Car Rental.
  • A rental agreement/documentation is completed for confirmed bookings.
  • Customers are responsible for the vehicle during the rental period as per the agreement.
  • Pickup/drop arrangements can be discussed during booking.

FAQ

Are the cars available for self-drive?

Yes. Self-drive is available for the listed vehicles, subject to eligibility and documentation.

Can I rent a car with a driver?

Yes. Driver service is available at an additional charge of ₹500/day.

Is the booking confirmed immediately after submitting the form?

No. The request is first checked for actual vehicle availability. Final confirmation is done by Samay Car Rental.

How many kilometres are included?

The standard package includes 300 KM per day. Additional kilometres are charged according to the selected car.

Do NRI customers need a security deposit?

For the listed rental terms, no security deposit is required for NRI customers.

What is required from Indian customers?

A two-wheeler and its RC book are required as security, subject to the rental agreement.

Do you provide cars for weddings?

Yes. Separate wedding and special-event cars are available. Submit an inquiry and our team will share options and pricing on WhatsApp.

Can I request a car that is not listed?

Yes. Select Other Requirement and enter the car/model you need.
